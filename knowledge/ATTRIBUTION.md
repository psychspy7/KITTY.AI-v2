# Public reference attribution

Source: Salesforce AI Research WikiText103 raw, https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext . The pinned source revision and preparation size are recorded in manifest.json after a verified import. Original Wikipedia contributors; CC BY-SA3.0 and GFDL. Adaptations: whitespace normalization, removal of WikiText punctuation markers, deduplication, UTF8 truncation at the byte budget, packing, keyword indexing and retrieval excerpts. Each packed article retains its individual Wikipedia source URL. No user conversations were used.

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ and https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html . Original research: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843 . Corpus preparation scripts are included so the adapted library can be reproduced and attributed. Corpus date is historical; it is not a live-web search index. Neither storing it nor including retrieved snippets fine-tunes the API provider's model.
